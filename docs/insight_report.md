# Country Intelligence: EDA Findings

1. **Population scale:** India and China are the two most populous countries in this dataset, at about 1.42 billion and 1.41 billion people.

2. **GDP per capita:** The United States ranks highest, followed by the Netherlands and the United Arab Emirates.

3. **Development gap:** Among the 25 most populous countries, the Democratic Republic of the Congo and Ethiopia have the lowest GDP per capita in this dataset.

4. **Urbanization and internet use:** These indicators have a positive correlation of 0.78 among countries with both values recorded.

5. **GDP per capita and internet use:** Their Spearman correlation is 0.91, a strong positive association in this dataset.

6. **Literacy and GDP per capita:** Their Spearman correlation is 0.81 among countries with both values recorded.

## Notes

- Correlation describes association; it does not establish cause and effect.
- Analyses use the available values. Blank indicator values are unknown and are not treated as zero.
- Check the source and estimate year for each indicator before making comparisons across years or editions.
